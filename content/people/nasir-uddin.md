## Biography

Nasir Uddin, also known as U Pho Khaing, was an influential Muslim community leader associated with Akyab. Historical records identify him as a member of Burma's legislature representing Akyab West during the early parliamentary period. He was listed as a Member of the Legislative Council in 1947.

Nasir Uddin was part of the early generation of Muslim political leaders who participated in Burma's parliamentary system following independence. His representation of Akyab West is significant in the history of Muslim political participation in Arakan. Historical accounts also describe U Pho Khaing as active in the political movement in Arakan during the struggle for independence.
