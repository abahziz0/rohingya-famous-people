## Biography

Aung Zaw Win is a Rohingya businessman and politician who served as a member of Myanmar's Parliament representing Maungdaw. He was elected during the 2010 general election and served in the Union Parliament during the 2011–2016 parliamentary period. Contemporary reporting identifies him as an Upper House representative from Maungdaw.

During his parliamentary period, Aung Zaw Win raised issues concerning Maungdaw residents. Parliamentary records cited in academic research show that he raised a question concerning the issuance of national registration cards to Maungdaw residents in September 2011. He also met local residents in Maungdaw and discussed development and identification issues.
