## Biography

M. A. Subahan was a member of Burma's Parliament representing the combined Maungdaw and Buthidaung area in the Upper House during the parliamentary period. Historical lists of Rohingya political representation identify him as an Upper House member in 1960.

His significance is primarily connected to the period when Rohingya and other Muslim representatives from northern Arakan participated in Burma's parliamentary system. He represented the combined Maungdaw–Buthidaung area during the 1960 parliamentary period.
