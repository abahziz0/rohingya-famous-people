## Biography

U Shwe Yat was a social worker and politician who represented Akyab in Burma's 1990 general election. Historical election records list him among the Muslim/Rohingya representatives elected during the 1990 multi-party election.

His significance is connected to the brief restoration of Rohingya political representation during the 1990 election. Historical records list U Shwe Yat as the representative for Akyab, alongside Rohingya representatives elected from Maungdaw and Buthidaung constituencies.
