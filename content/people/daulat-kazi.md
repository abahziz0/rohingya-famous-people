## Biography

Daulat Kazi was a distinguished Bengali poet who served in the royal court of the Mrauk U Kingdom (present-day Rakhine State, Myanmar). Born in 1600 in Sultanpur, Chittagong region (now Bangladesh), he moved to Arakan seeking recognition for his literary talent.

Under the patronage of Ashraf Khan, a Sufi officer under King Thiri Thudhamma (reigned 1622–1638), Daulat Kazi began composing Satimayna Lor Chandrani, an adaptation of Avadhi folk narratives into Bengali verse. He passed away in 1638 before completing it, and the work was later finished by the poet Alaol.

Daulat Kazi is remembered as one of the earliest Bengali poets in the Arakan court, contributing to the rich cultural and literary heritage of medieval Arakan. His works represent a significant fusion of Bengali literary traditions and Arakanese patronage.
