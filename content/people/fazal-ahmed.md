## Biography

Fazal Ahmed is a prominent Rohingya lawyer and former politician from Maungdaw, Arakan. As a senior member and central leader of the National Democratic Party for Human Rights (NDPHR), he was elected as a Member of Parliament (MP-elect) for the Maungdaw-2 constituency in the 1990 general election, becoming one of only four Rohingya MPs to win seats.

Ahmed devoted his professional life to pursuing equality and justice, serving in government offices before qualifying as a Higher Grade Pleader (HGP) and practicing law in Maungdaw. He provided legal support to Rohingya citizens facing discrimination and citizenship denials. As a parliamentarian-elect, he worked to promote the recognition of the Rohingya as an ethnic group of Myanmar and demanded the restoration of their citizenship rights. He advocated for peaceful coexistence between Rakhine Buddhists and Rohingya Muslims.

After the NDPHR was banned in 1992, Fazal Ahmed was arrested, tortured, and sentenced to five years in prison, part of a broader campaign to silence Rohingya democratic representation. He remains a symbol of lawful resistance.
