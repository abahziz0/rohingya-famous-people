## Biography

Dr. Abdu Rahim, also known as Dr. Chit Maung, was a Rohingya political figure who represented Maungdaw during the BSPP period. Historical records identify him as a member of the 1974 Hluttaw, representing Maungdaw.

Rahim's political significance comes from his participation in Burma's national political system as a representative from a Rohingya-majority area. His election in 1974 demonstrates that Rohingya representatives continued to hold parliamentary positions during the early BSPP period.
