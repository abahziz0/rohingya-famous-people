## Biography

Abul Khair was a Rohingya politician from Maungdaw, Arakan, born in 1919. He became involved in public affairs and national politics during Burma's parliamentary period after independence. He was elected to Burma's Union Parliament representing Maungdaw during the democratic parliamentary era before the 1962 military coup.

As a parliamentarian, Abul Khair represented the population of northern Arakan during a period when Rohingya representatives participated in Burma's national political system. Historical accounts identify him among the Rohingya political leaders who sought political representation and development for their communities before the military takeover in 1962. He remained a respected political figure after parliamentary politics were abolished.
