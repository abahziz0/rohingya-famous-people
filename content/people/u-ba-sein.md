## Biography

U Ba Sein is a Rohingya writer and community figure known for documenting and sharing information about the Rohingya crisis. He is best recognized as the founder of Rohingya Blogger, one of the early online platforms used to report on human rights abuses and the situation of Rohingya in Arakan (Rakhine State).

U Ba Sein worked as a civil servant in Myanmar’s Ministry of Co-operatives before later fleeing the country due to increasing discrimination and persecution against Rohingya.

In exile in the United Kingdom, he became active in advocacy and information work focused on Rohingya rights.

He has provided first-hand testimony about historical military operations against Rohingya, including the 1978 Naga Min (King Dragon) operation. His accounts have been cited in academic and policy discussions on Rohingya persecution and have helped researchers, journalists, and human rights groups understand patterns of abuse in Rakhine State.

In 2005, he founded the Rohingya Blogger website, which became a source of news, statements, and documentation related to the Rohingya community. Through writing and information-sharing, he contributed to raising international awareness about the Rohingya situation.
