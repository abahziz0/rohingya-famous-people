## Biography

Jaivet Ealom is a Rohingya refugee from Myanmar who escaped persecution, endured detention, and found safety in Canada. He completed a double major in economics and political science at the University of Toronto.

His memoir, Escape from Manus Prison: One Man’s Daring Quest for Freedom, documents his journey and highlights the broader struggles of statelessness and displacement for the Rohingya. Ealom co-founded the Rohingya Centre of Canada and Northern Lights Canada, which support Rohingya newcomers with integration. He also serves as a co-spokesperson for the Convening Committee to form the Rohingya Consultative Council.
