## Biography

Rashid Ahmed, also known as U Tin Maung and Mayu Rashid, was a Rohingya community elder from Maungdaw. He was the son of Zahir Miah and Fatema Khatun and was described by the European Rohingya Council as a respected elder of Quarter 4 in Maungdaw Township.

After being forced into exile in 1994, Rashid Ahmed settled in Chittagong, Bangladesh. According to the European Rohingya Council, he continued working for the Rohingya cause while living in Bangladesh. He remained connected to the community and supported the work of Rohingya organizations and activists. He died in Chittagong on 16 September 2021, aged 73.
