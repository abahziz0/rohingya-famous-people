## Biography

Shaykh Abdul Wali al‑Arkani, also called Abu Muʿāḍ, was born in 1983 in Mislafah, Mecca, to a Rohingya-origin family from Arakan (Rakhine State, Myanmar). He memorized the Quran early, guided by his father and other teachers, beginning formal recitation studies at age 14, particularly under notable scholars including Shaykh Harun Dahir, the late Shaykh Muhammad Kabir, and Shaykh Muhammad Idriss Abbass.

He joined the Al-Arqam Ibn Abu Al-Arqam Institute at Masjid al‑Haram and graduated in 1997. His first imamate role was at age 13 in Mecca at the mosque of Shaykh Muhammad Saleh Jamal. After serving in various Mecca mosques, he became Imam of Khādim al-Haramayn ash-Sharifayn in Jeddah, preaching and reciting Taraweeh and regular prayers.

He has been officially invited to lead prayers alongside esteemed reciters such as Mishary Rashid al-Afasy in Ajman, UAE, an honor reflecting his rising reputation. His distinctive, melodious Quran recitations are recorded and broadcast across major Arab channels in Saudi Arabia, Abu Dhabi, and Bahrain, and are available on platforms like Assabile.com and Quran Central.
