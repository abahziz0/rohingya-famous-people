## Biography

Rashid was an educator and politician who represented Maungdaw North in Burma's Parliament. Historical records list him as a member elected in 1960.

Rashid's significance lies in his participation in the parliamentary representation of northern Arakan. His election from Maungdaw North placed a representative from the Rohingya-majority region in Burma's national legislature during the final years before the 1962 military coup.
